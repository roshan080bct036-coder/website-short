IMAGES
======

Drop a portrait photo here named exactly:

    dubi-nanda-dhakal.jpg

Recommended: a vertical (portrait) photo, at least 640 x 800 px,
ideally 1000 x 1250 px. JPG or WebP (if WebP, also update the <img>
src in index.html).

Until a file with that name exists, the site shows a typographic
placeholder (the Devanagari letter "du" over the initials DND) in the
hero. Everything else works without it.

A good source is the photo on the Central Department of Linguistics
faculty page: https://cdltu.edu.np/dubi-nanda-dhakal/
